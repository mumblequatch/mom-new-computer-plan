Your new computer: a plan

For you to read and comment on. Nothing is bought or decided yet.

Why a new one

Your MacBook Pro is from 2015. Apple stopped sending security fixes for the newest system it can run in July 2024, and the Brave browser and the AI assistant I'd want you to have no longer install on it. It can be patched with unofficial software, but then I'd have to handle every update by hand, and it still wouldn't be as safe as a Mac Apple supports.

What I'd suggest

MacBook Air, 13-inch, with the M5 chip (16 GB memory, 512 GB storage). It's light, quiet, the battery lasts all day, and Apple will keep it updated for years.

What would be on it

  • Brave, for the web. It blocks ads and trackers on its own.
  • Mail, Apple's built-in email, set up with your current account.
  • An AI digital assistant capable of anything a Claude or Muse can do: research, help with the computer, making appointments, and more.
  • Pages, for writing documents. It opens and saves Word files.
  • A password manager (Apple Passwords or Bitwarden) to remember every password for you.
  • AnyDesk, so I can see your screen and fix things from my house when you ask.

How it stays secure

  • Updates install themselves.
  • The drive is encrypted, so a lost laptop doesn't expose your files.
  • Every password lives in the password manager, and each one is different.
  • Only I can connect remotely, and you never have to type a code to let me in.

What you'd do

Almost nothing. I set it up and move your files over, and you sign in to your email and Apple account when I ask.

Questions for you

  • Is there anything else you use on your computer now that you'd want to keep?
  • Which email address do you use most?
  • Do you exchange Word documents with anyone?
